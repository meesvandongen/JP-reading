雨ニモマケズ
> Not giving in to the rain,
風ニモマケズ
> not giving in to the wind,
雪ニモ夏ノ暑サニモマケヌ
> giving in neither to the snow nor to the summer heat,
丈夫ナカラダヲモチ
> with a strong, sound body,
慾ハナク
> without greed,
決シテ瞋ラズ
> never angry,
イツモシヅカニワラッテヰル
> always smiling quietly,
一日ニ玄米四合ト
> eating four gō of brown rice a day,
味噌ト少《すこ》シノ野菜ヲタベ
> with miso and a few vegetables,
アラユルコトヲ
> in all things
ジブンヲカンジョウニ入レズニ
> leaving myself out of the reckoning,
ヨクミキキシワカリ
> looking and listening well, and understanding,
ソシテワスレズ
> and not forgetting;
野原ノ松ノ林ノ䕃《かげ》ノ
> in the shade of the pine wood on the plain,
小サナ萓ブキノ小屋ニヰテ
> living in a little thatched hut;
東ニ病気ノコドモアレバ
> if there is a sick child to the east,
行ッテ看病シテヤリ
> going and nursing it;
西ニツカレタ母アレバ
> if there is a tired mother to the west,
行ッテソノ稲ノ朿ヲ負ヒ
> going and carrying her sheaves of rice;
南ニ死ニサウナ人アレバ
> if there is someone dying to the south,
行ッテコハガラナクテモイヽトイヒ
> going and telling them there is no need to be afraid;
北ニケンクヮヤソショウガアレバ
> if there is a quarrel or a lawsuit to the north,
ツマラナイカラヤメロトイヒ
> telling them to stop, it isn't worth it;
ヒドリノトキハナミダヲナガシ
> shedding tears in times of drought,
サムサノナツハオロオロアルキ
> walking about helplessly in a cold summer,
ミンナニデクノボートヨバレ
> called a blockhead by everyone,
ホメラレモセズ
> never praised,
クニモサレズ
> and no trouble to anyone:
サウイフモノニ
> that is the kind of person
ワタシハナリタイ
> I want to be.
