**Masu-stem + ながら** means "while doing": one person does two actions at the same time. 音楽を聞きながら勉強する = study while listening to music.

## Formation

| Verb | Masu-stem | + ながら |
| ---- | --------- | -------- |
| 聞く | 聞き | 聞きながら |
| 食べる | 食べ | 食べながら |
| 歩く | 歩き | 歩きながら |
| する | し | しながら |
| 来る | 来 (き) | 来ながら |

**Pattern:** [secondary action] ながら [main action]

The **main action comes last**, and it sets the tense and politeness. The ながら clause is the background or accompanying action.

## Examples

- 音楽を聞きながら勉強します。 — I study while listening to music.
- テレビを見ながらご飯を食べないで。 — Don't eat while watching TV.
- 父はいつも新聞を読みながら朝ご飯を食べる。 — My father always eats breakfast while reading the newspaper.
- 歩きながらスマホを使うのは危ないです。 — Using your phone while walking is dangerous.
- 彼女は笑いながら話していた。 — She was talking and laughing at the same time.
- 働きながら大学に通っています。 — I'm going to university while working.
- コーヒーでも飲みながら話しましょう。 — Let's talk over a coffee or something.

## Which action is main?

Compare:

| Sentence | Focus |
| -------- | ----- |
| 音楽を聞きながら勉強する。 | studying is the main activity; music is in the background |
| 勉強しながら音楽を聞く。 | listening to music is the focus; studying is secondary |

## Same subject only

Both actions must be done by **the same person**. For "while someone else is doing", use 間 (あいだ) or 間に: 母が料理している間、私は掃除した (While my mother was cooking, I cleaned).

## Longer periods

ながら can describe ongoing situations over a long time, not just moment-to-moment: アルバイトをしながら学校に通う (go to school while working part time).

## "Although": a second meaning

With state verbs or adjectives, ながら(も) can mean "although, despite": 残念ながら (unfortunately, lit. "while it's regrettable"), 知っていながら、何も言わなかった (Although he knew, he said nothing). This usage is more advanced and formal; the "while doing" meaning is the one to learn first.

## Pitfalls

- Use only action verbs that take time. Instantaneous verbs (着く, 死ぬ) don't work with ながら in the "while" sense.
- Do not use the te-form or dictionary form: 聞きながら, not 聞くながら.
- For "during" with a noun, use 中 (ちゅう) or の間: 食事中, 授業の間.
