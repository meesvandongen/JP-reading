**なら** means "if it is the case that X", "if you're talking about X". It typically picks up something the listener said or a situation, and gives advice, an opinion or a decision based on it.

## Formation

| Before | Example |
| ------ | ------- |
| Noun | 日本なら |
| Na-adjective | 静かなら |
| I-adjective | 高いなら |
| Verb (dictionary) | 行くなら |
| Verb (past) | 行ったなら |
| Verb (negative) | 行かないなら |

Note that nouns and na-adjectives attach directly (no だ). In casual speech, なら is often expanded to **んなら** or **のなら** after verbs: 行くんなら.

## Examples

- 「京都に行きたいんです。」「京都なら、秋がいいですよ。」 — "I want to go to Kyoto." "If it's Kyoto, autumn is good."
- 日本へ行くなら、JRパスを買ったほうがいい。 — If you're going to Japan, you should buy a JR Pass.
- 寒いなら、窓を閉めましょうか。 — If you're cold, shall I close the window?
- 「ペンありますか。」「ペンなら、机の上にありますよ。」 — "Do you have a pen?" "If it's a pen you want, there's one on the desk."
- お酒を飲むなら、運転しないでください。 — If you're going to drink, please don't drive.
- そんなに嫌なら、やめてもいいよ。 — If you hate it that much, you can stop.
- 君がそう言うなら、信じるよ。 — If you say so, I'll believe you.

## Topic-picking なら

With a noun, なら works almost like a topic marker: "speaking of X". It highlights X as the best or relevant choice: 寿司なら、あの店が一番だ (When it comes to sushi, that shop is the best).

## Order of events

なら does **not** imply that the condition happens first. Compare:

| Sentence | Meaning |
| -------- | ------- |
| 日本に行ったら、カメラを買う。 | I'll buy a camera once I'm in Japan. |
| 日本に行くなら、カメラを買う。 | If I'm going to Japan, I'll buy a camera (beforehand). |

This is why なら fits advice about preparation: 行くなら、予約したほうがいい (If you're going, you'd better book).

## Comparing the conditionals

- **と:** automatic consequence.
- **ば:** necessary condition, general truth.
- **たら:** once X happens.
- **なら:** given X (as information), here's my response.

## Pitfalls

- なら doesn't suit natural laws or automatic results: 春になるなら、桜が咲く sounds wrong; use 春になると.
- なら with a noun can also mean "if (it were)": 私なら、そうしない (If I were you, I wouldn't do that).
- Don't confuse with the ば-form of the copula: 静かならば is just a more formal なら.
