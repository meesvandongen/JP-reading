**で** answers "where (does it happen)?", "with what?" and "by what means?". It attaches to nouns.

## Main uses

| Use | Example | Meaning |
| --- | ------- | ------- |
| Place of action | 図書館で勉強する | study at the library |
| Means, tool | 箸で食べる | eat with chopsticks |
| Transport | バスで行く | go by bus |
| Language | 日本語で話す | speak in Japanese |
| Material | 木で作る | make from wood |
| Cause, reason | 風邪で休む | be off because of a cold |
| Scope, limit | クラスで一番 | the best in the class |
| Total, amount | 全部で千円 | a thousand yen in total |
| Time limit | 一時間で終わる | finish in an hour |

## Examples

- 毎日、駅前のカフェで朝ご飯を食べます。 — Every day I have breakfast at a café in front of the station.
- ボールペンで名前を書いてください。 — Please write your name with a ballpoint pen.
- 会社まで自転車で十五分です。 — It's fifteen minutes to the office by bike.
- この漢字は日本語で何と読みますか。 — How do you read this kanji in Japanese?
- 台風で電車が止まった。 — The trains stopped because of the typhoon.
- 富士山は日本で一番高い山です。 — Mt. Fuji is the highest mountain in Japan.
- 三人で旅行に行きました。 — The three of us went on a trip.

## で vs に for places

This is the biggest pitfall. **に** marks where something **is** (ある, いる, 住む). **で** marks where something **happens** (eat, work, play, buy).

| Sentence | Meaning |
| -------- | ------- |
| 公園に子供がいる。 | There are children in the park. |
| 公園で子供が遊んでいる。 | Children are playing in the park. |

Events take で even with ある: 体育館でコンサートがある (There's a concert in the gym), because a concert "takes place" rather than "exists".

## Not to be confused with

- **で as the te-form of だ:** 彼は学生で、二十歳です (He is a student and is twenty). This is the copula, not the particle. See [Te-form](#/grammar/te-form).
- **では:** で + は, as in 日本では (in Japan, as for), or the formal negative ではありません.
- **で for people as a group:** 一人で (alone), 家族で (as a family), みんなで (all together).

## Pitfalls

- Walking or running **through** a place takes を, not で: 公園を走る. See [Particle を](#/grammar/particle-wo).
- "On foot" is 歩いて, not 足で.
