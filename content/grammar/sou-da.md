There are two separate grammar patterns written **そうだ**, and they attach differently:

1. **Appearance (様態)**: stem + そうだ = "looks like", "seems about to". Based on what you see.
2. **Hearsay (伝聞)**: plain form + そうだ = "I heard that", "apparently". Based on what you were told.

## Formation

| Type | Appearance: "looks" | Hearsay: "I heard" |
| ---- | ------------------- | ------------------ |
| Verb | masu-stem + そう: 降りそう | plain + そう: 降るそう |
| I-adjective | drop い: 美味しそう | 美味しいそう |
| Na-adjective | stem: 元気そう | 元気だそう |
| Noun | (not used) | 学生だそう |
| いい | よさそう | いいそう |
| ない | なさそう | ないそう |

Appearance そう works like a **na-adjective**: 美味しそうなケーキ (a delicious-looking cake), 元気そうに見える.

Hearsay そう has only the forms そうだ / そうです; it doesn't take な, past or negative.

## Appearance examples

- このケーキ、美味しそう！ — This cake looks delicious!
- 空が暗いですね。雨が降りそうです。 — The sky is dark. It looks like it's going to rain.
- 田中さんは今日、元気がなさそうだ。 — Mr. Tanaka seems down today.
- 棚から本が落ちそうになった。 — A book almost fell off the shelf.

## Hearsay examples

- 天気予報によると、明日は雨が降るそうです。 — According to the forecast, it's going to rain tomorrow.
- 山田さんは来月結婚するそうだ。 — I heard Ms. Yamada is getting married next month.
- あの店のラーメンはとても美味しいそうですよ。 — Apparently the ramen at that shop is very good.

## Telling them apart

| Sentence | Meaning |
| -------- | ------- |
| 雨が降りそうだ | It looks like it's about to rain (I can see the clouds). |
| 雨が降るそうだ | I heard it's going to rain (someone told me). |

The source of hearsay is often marked with **によると** or **の話では**.

## Appearance with verbs: "about to"

With verbs, appearance そう often means something is on the verge of happening: 泣きそう (about to cry), 死にそう (feel like I'm dying), 倒れそう (about to fall).

## Negatives

- Appearance with adjectives: 美味しくなさそう (doesn't look tasty), or 美味しそうじゃない.
- Appearance with verbs: 降りそうにない / 降りそうもない (doesn't look like it will rain).

## Pitfalls

- Don't use appearance そう for things that are obvious at a glance: かわいそう means "pitiful", not "looks cute"! Just say かわいい. Likewise for colours: 赤そう is wrong.
- いい and ない become よさそう and なさそう.
- Appearance そう does not attach to nouns; use [みたい / ようだ](#/grammar/you-da) instead: 学生みたい.
