**から** means "from" and **まで** means "until" or "as far as". They mark the start and end points of a range in time or space, and they often appear as a pair.

## Formation

| Pattern | Example | Meaning |
| ------- | ------- | ------- |
| Time + から | 九時から | from nine o'clock |
| Time + まで | 五時まで | until five |
| Place + から | 駅から | from the station |
| Place + まで | 空港まで | as far as the airport |
| Person + から | 友達から | from a friend |
| X から Y まで | 月曜日から金曜日まで | from Monday to Friday |
| Time + までに | 五時までに | by five (deadline) |

## Examples

- 銀行は九時から三時までです。 — The bank is open from nine to three.
- 家から駅まで歩いて十分です。 — It's a ten-minute walk from my house to the station.
- これは母から届いた手紙です。 — This is a letter that came from my mother.
- 夏休みは来週から始まります。 — Summer vacation starts next week.
- 終点まで乗ってください。 — Please ride to the last stop.
- レポートは金曜日までに出してください。 — Please hand in the report by Friday.
- 雨がやむまで、ここで待ちましょう。 — Let's wait here until the rain stops.

## まで vs までに

This is the most common pitfall.

- **まで** = "until": the action **continues** up to that point. 五時まで働く (work until five).
- **までに** = "by": the action happens **at some point before** the deadline. 五時までに帰る (be back by five).

## から with people

から marks the source of something received: 先生から聞いた (heard it from the teacher). With もらう and 借りる, both に and から work: 友達に／から本を借りた. If the source is an organisation, から is preferred: 会社から電話があった.

## Verbs with から and まで

Both can follow verbs. まで after the dictionary form means "until (something happens)": 帰るまで (until I get home). から after the te-form means "after doing": 食べてから (after eating). See [Mae ni / ato de](#/grammar/mae-ni-ato-de).

Do not confuse "from" から with the **reason** から, which follows a full clause: 暑いから窓を開けた (I opened the window because it was hot). See [Kara / node](#/grammar/kara-node).

## Other uses of まで

まで can mean "even": 子供まで知っている (Even children know it). The nuance is that something has gone further than expected.

## Pitfalls

- から and まで can be followed by other particles: 東京からの電車 (the train from Tokyo), 駅までは遠い (as far as the station, it's far).
- Material that is visibly transformed takes から: 米から酒を作る (make sake from rice). Visible materials take で: 木で机を作る.
