**に** is one of the busiest particles. Its core idea is a **point** or **target**: a point in time, a place something exists at, a destination, or a person something goes to.

## Main uses

| Use | Example | Meaning |
| --- | ------- | ------- |
| Specific time | 七時に起きる | get up at seven |
| Location of existence | 部屋に猫がいる | there's a cat in the room |
| Destination | 学校に行く | go to school |
| Recipient / target | 友達に電話する | call a friend |
| Purpose of movement | 買い物に行く | go shopping |
| Result of change | 医者になる | become a doctor |
| Agent (passive, causative) | 先生に褒められた | was praised by the teacher |
| Frequency | 週に二回 | twice a week |

## Examples

- 毎朝六時に起きます。 — I get up at six every morning.
- 駅の前に本屋があります。 — There is a bookshop in front of the station.
- 来週、京都に行きます。 — I'm going to Kyoto next week.
- 母に花をあげました。 — I gave my mother flowers.
- 公園に写真を撮りに行きました。 — I went to the park to take photos.
- 姉は去年、看護師になりました。 — My sister became a nurse last year.
- 電車に乗って、席に座った。 — I got on the train and sat in a seat.

## Time: when to use it

Use に with **specific** times: clock times, dates, days of the week, years (三時に, 五月十日に, 月曜日に). Do **not** use に with relative time words: 今日, 明日, 昨日, 毎日, 来週, 今. So 明日行きます, not 明日に行きます. Seasons and parts of the day (朝, 夏) can go either way.

## に vs で for location

に marks where something **exists** (with ある, いる, 住む, 座る). で marks where an **action** happens. Compare:

| Sentence | Meaning |
| -------- | ------- |
| 図書館にいます。 | I'm at the library. |
| 図書館で勉強します。 | I study at the library. |

## に vs へ for direction

Both can mark a destination. に emphasises arrival at the point, へ the direction. In everyday speech, に is more common. See [Particle へ](#/grammar/particle-he).

## Purpose: verb stem + に

With motion verbs (行く, 来る, 帰る), the masu-stem + に shows purpose: 映画を見に行く (go to see a movie), 昼ご飯を食べに帰る (go home to eat lunch).

## Pitfalls

- 会う (meet) and 乗る (ride) take に, not を: 友達に会う, バスに乗る.
- In passive and causative sentences, に marks the "by" person, which surprises English speakers. See [Passive](#/grammar/passive).
