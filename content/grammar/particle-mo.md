**も** means "also", "too" or "as well". It replaces は, が and を, and attaches after other particles.

## Formation

| Pattern | Example | Meaning |
| ------- | ------- | ------- |
| N も (replaces は/が/を) | 私も行く | I'll go too |
| N + particle + も | 東京にも行った | went to Tokyo too |
| N も N も | 犬も猫も好き | like both dogs and cats |
| N も + negative | 犬も猫も好きじゃない | like neither dogs nor cats |
| Question word + も + negative | 何も食べない | don't eat anything |
| Number + も | 三時間も待った | waited as much as three hours |

## Examples

- 私も学生です。 — I'm a student too.
- 昨日は雨でした。今日も雨です。 — It rained yesterday. It's raining today too.
- 兄はピアノもギターも弾けます。 — My older brother can play both the piano and the guitar.
- 冷蔵庫に何もありません。 — There's nothing in the fridge.
- 日曜日はどこにも行きませんでした。 — I didn't go anywhere on Sunday.
- 駅で一時間も待ちました。 — I waited at the station for a whole hour.
- 誰も知らなかった。 — Nobody knew.

## Replacing vs stacking

も **replaces** は, が and を: 私は → 私も, 本を → 本も. With other particles it is added **after**: 京都にも, 友達とも, 学校でも. Saying 私はも or 本をも is wrong (をも exists only in literary style).

## Question words + も

With a negative verb, question word + も means "not any":

| Question word | + も + negative |
| ------------- | --------------- |
| 何 (what) | 何も (nothing) |
| 誰 (who) | 誰も (nobody) |
| どこ (where) | どこにも / どこへも (nowhere) |
| いつ (when) | いつも is "always" (positive!) |

Note that いつも is positive ("always") and どれも / どちらも can be positive ("all of them"). For "anyone/anything" in positive sentences, Japanese uses でも: 何でもいい (anything is fine), 誰でもできる (anyone can do it).

## Emphasis with numbers

After a number, も expresses that the amount is surprisingly large: 十杯も飲んだ (drank as many as ten cups). With a negative, it says "not even": 一円もない (I don't have even one yen).

## Pitfalls

- Put も directly after the thing that is "also": 私もりんごを食べた (I too ate an apple) vs 私はりんごも食べた (I ate an apple too, besides other things).
- 何も is pronounced なにも; 何でも is なんでも.
