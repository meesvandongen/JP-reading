The **causative-passive** combines the [causative](#/grammar/causative) ("make someone do") and the [passive](#/grammar/passive) ("be done to"). The result means **"be made to do"**: the subject was forced to do something they didn't want to. 私は母に野菜を食べさせられた = I was made to eat vegetables by my mother.

## Formation

Make the causative, then make that verb passive: させる → させられる.

| Group | Causative | Causative-passive | Short form (godan) |
| ----- | --------- | ----------------- | ------------------ |
| 書く | 書かせる | 書かせられる | 書かされる |
| 飲む | 飲ませる | 飲ませられる | 飲まされる |
| 待つ | 待たせる | 待たせられる | 待たされる |
| 行く | 行かせる | 行かせられる | 行かされる |
| 話す | 話させる | 話させられる | (no short form) |
| 食べる | 食べさせる | 食べさせられる | |
| する | させる | させられる | |
| 来る | 来させる | 来させられる (こさせられる) | |

For godan verbs, the **short form** (a-sound + される) is the usual spoken choice: 飲まされる, 待たされる. Verbs ending in す cannot use the short form, because さされる would be awkward, so 話させられる is used.

## Pattern

**[Forced person] は [person who forces] に [object を] verb-causative-passive**

## Examples

- 子供のころ、毎日ピアノを練習させられた。 — As a child, I was made to practise the piano every day.
- 病院で一時間も待たされました。 — I was kept waiting at the hospital for a whole hour.
- 飲み会で、先輩にお酒を飲まされた。 — I was made to drink by my seniors at the party.
- 先生に教室を掃除させられた。 — I was made to clean the classroom by the teacher.
- 会議でみんなの前でスピーチをさせられた。 — I was made to give a speech in front of everyone at the meeting.
- 彼の話には、いつも考えさせられる。 — His talks always make me think.

## Nuance

The causative-passive almost always implies the subject **didn't want** to do it, or did it reluctantly. Compare:

| Sentence | Meaning |
| -------- | ------- |
| 母は私に皿を洗わせた。 | My mother made me wash the dishes. (mother's viewpoint) |
| 私は母に皿を洗わされた。 | I was made to wash the dishes by my mother. (my viewpoint, complaint) |
| 母に皿を洗わせてもらった。 | My mother let me wash the dishes. (a favour, positive) |

An exception is with emotion and thought verbs, where it can be neutral or positive: 感動させられた (I was deeply moved), 考えさせられる (it makes you think).

## Pitfalls

- Don't mix the forms: 飲ませされる is wrong. Use 飲ませられる or 飲まされる.
- The person who forced the action is marked with に, just like in the passive.
- The short form is only for godan verbs. 食べさされる is dialectal and non-standard.
