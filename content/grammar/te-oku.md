**ておく** (te-form + おく, "put, place") means doing something **in advance** or **for a future purpose**, or **leaving** something in a certain state. The idea is that the action's result is "put aside" for later.

## Formation

Te-form + おく. おく is a godan verb (おきます, おいた, おいて).

| | Full | Casual |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Present | 買っておく | 買っとく |
| Past | 買っておいた | 買っといた |
| Request | 買っておいてください | 買っといて |
| Volitional | 買っておこう | 買っとこう |
| With で | 読んでおく | 読んどく |

In speech, ておく contracts to **とく** and でおく to **どく**.

## Uses

**1. Preparation in advance**

- 旅行の前に、ホテルを予約しておきます。 — I'll book a hotel before the trip.
- 明日テストだから、今夜よく復習しておこう。 — The test is tomorrow, so I'll review well tonight.
- パーティーのために飲み物を買っておいた。 — I bought drinks in advance for the party.

**2. Doing something now for later convenience**

- 使ったら、元の場所に戻しておいてください。 — After you use it, please put it back where it was.
- 忘れないように、メモしておきます。 — I'll make a note so I don't forget.

**3. Leaving something as it is**

- 窓は開けておいてください。 — Please leave the window open.
- 疲れているみたいだから、寝かせておこう。 — He seems tired, so let's let him sleep.

**Casual**

- これ、やっとくね。 — I'll take care of this.

## ておく vs てある

| ておく | てある |
| ------ | ------ |
| focus on doing the preparation | focus on the resulting state |
| ビールを冷やしておく (I'll chill the beer) | ビールが冷やしてある (The beer has been chilled) |
| the doer is the subject | the doer is backgrounded |

Often they come in pairs: 昨日ビールを冷やしておいたから、冷蔵庫に冷やしてあるよ (I chilled the beer yesterday, so there's some cold in the fridge).

## Offering and promising

ておきます often has a reassuring, responsible feel: 私が伝えておきます (I'll pass it on for you), 確認しておきます (I'll check on that). It is very common in business Japanese.

## Pitfalls

- ておく needs a verb you can control. 雨が降っておく is wrong.
- The casual forms とく / どく are easy to misread when listening: 言っとく = 言っておく ("I'm telling you now"), 置いとく = 置いておく.
- おく is usually written in hiragana in this pattern, not 置く.
