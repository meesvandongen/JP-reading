**前に** (mae ni) means "before" and **後で** (ato de) means "after". The key point is which verb form goes in front: **dictionary form** before 前に, **ta-form** before 後で.

## Formation

| Pattern | Rule | Example |
| ------- | ---- | ------- |
| Verb (dictionary) + 前に | before doing | 寝る前に |
| Noun + の + 前に | before (event) | 食事の前に |
| Verb (ta-form) + 後で | after doing | 食べた後で |
| Noun + の + 後で | after (event) | 授業の後で |
| Verb (te-form) + から | after doing, then | 食べてから |
| Time + 前に / 後で | ago / later | 三年前に, 一時間後に |

## Examples

- 寝る前に歯を磨きます。 — I brush my teeth before going to bed.
- 食事の前に手を洗ってください。 — Please wash your hands before the meal.
- 宿題をした後で、テレビを見た。 — After doing my homework, I watched TV.
- 授業の後で、図書館に行きます。 — I'm going to the library after class.
- 日本に来る前に、少し日本語を勉強しました。 — I studied a little Japanese before coming to Japan.
- 仕事が終わってから、飲みに行こう。 — Let's go for a drink after work is finished.
- 三年前に、この町に引っ越しました。 — I moved to this town three years ago.

## Why dictionary form before 前に?

Before 前に, the action has **not yet happened**, so the tense is always non-past, even when the sentence is in the past: 日本に来る前に勉強した (I studied before I came to Japan), never 来た前に.

Similarly, the action before 後で has **already happened**, so it is always ta-form, even in future sentences: 食べた後で行きます (I'll go after I've eaten).

## 後で vs てから

Both mean "after", but:

- **てから** emphasises sequence, and often that the first action is a necessary step: 手を洗ってから食べる (wash hands first, then eat). Also "since": 日本に来てから三年になる (It's been three years since I came to Japan).
- **後で** simply places one event after another, and can be used for a vaguer "later on".

On its own, 後で means "later": 後で電話します (I'll call you later).

## 後で vs 後に

後に is a bit more formal and focuses on the point in time; 後で implies doing something in that later period. In everyday speech 後で is more common.

## Pitfalls

- In 後で, 後 is read あと. After a time word it is read ご: 一週間後 (いっしゅうかんご, a week later). 後ろ (うしろ) means "behind".
- Time word + 前 = "ago": 一週間前 (a week ago).
